\section{Reproduction and anonymisation}

The author authorises sharing of operational data after removal of company names, aircraft registrations and other identifying information. Reidentification mappings and identifiable originals are excluded. Anonymisation must preserve dates, exposure, event linkage and missingness needed to reproduce the stated analyses. The current local computational candidate reproduces the synthetic experiments and mathematical tests; this does not establish that the complete revised empirical package is already publicly accessible. Its data release requires verification of the anonymised inputs and their derived results. No superseded safety weighting, pseudocensoring, empirical AOG cost or 81-day replacement prescription is part of this supplement.
